## Brand & Style

The design system articulates an institutional paradigm centered on openness, institutional dignity, and public accountability ("Estado Abierto"). It rejects bureaucratic density in favor of civic clarity, balancing modern democratic transparency with republican sobriety.

### Aesthetic Persona & Tone
- **Dignified & Democratic:** Communicates sovereign dependability through balanced visual weight, clean structural grids, and purposeful symmetry.
- **Radically Accessible:** Prioritizes effortless scanning, legible hierarchy, and cognitive ease for citizens across all literacy levels and digital modalities.
- **Architectural Modernism:** Employs precise geometric proportions, subtle boundaries, and pure tonal contrast to emulate contemporary civic architecture—open, welcoming, yet enduring.

### Visual Style
The system adopts an evolved **Modern Corporate & Civic** framework with subtle tactile touches:
- Generous structural whitespace to convey institutional composure and unhurried access to critical public services.
- Micro-interactions characterized by deliberate, linear-out-slow transitions that feel reliable and steady rather than flashy.
- Clear structural division through crisp containment and hairline borders, ensuring that information density never compromises navigational clarity.

## Colors

The palette grounds civic identity in historical resonance while adhering strictly to WCAG contrast guidelines across dark interfaces.

### Semantic Hierarchy & Roles
- **Primary (`#E72421` - Rojo Cívico):** The anchoring color of authority, public verification, and state presence. Applied to main interactive mechanisms, primary actions, critical navigation, and active system states.
- **Secondary (`#CC4C40` - Carmesí Institucional):** A subdued, solemn red used with restraint. Reserved for urgent citizen notices, legal alerts, vital deadlines, and discrete sovereign markers. Never applied to bulk decorative elements.
- **Tertiary (`#006DC6` - Azul Complementario):** A refined blue designating institutional milestones, historical archives, public awards, and civic badges of honor.
- **Neutral (`#8C716D` - Gris Piedra Oscuro):** Delivers rigorous text legibility on dark surfaces, avoiding harsh pure black to maintain optical comfort during long sessions of legal or civic reading.

### Background & Surface Architecture
- **Canvas Base:** Dark-mode optimized surfaces serve as the primary ground to evoke civic transparency and high contrast.
- **Tonal Layers:** Used for canvas depth and module separation; defines non-interactive structural delimiters and table outlines.

## Typography

The typographic system pairs the structural clarity of **Inter** for programmatic headings with the modern tone of **Epilogue** for dense civic narratives and procedural forms.

### Hierarchy & Editorial Rules
- **Headlines (Inter):** Deliver authoritative anchoring. Tighter tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.02em`) preserves architectural solidity in executive titles, law banners, and section markers.
- **Reading Body (Epilogue):** Generous line-height (`1.5` to `1.55`) avoids visual fatigue when citizen users review policy briefs, decrees, or official documentation.
- **Labels & Microcopy (Epilogue Medium/SemiBold):** Slightly tracked out (`+0.01em` to `+0.03em`) for tags, civic process stamps, table column heads, and field labels to maintain instant legibility under varied environmental light.

## Layout & Spacing

The structural layout relies on an 8px base rhythm arranged into a strict 12-column responsive grid on desktop and a single/two-column stacking paradigm on mobile devices.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column layout with `2rem` outer margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Constrained to a `1280px` maximum content container to prevent horizontal drift in wide citizen monitors.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column layout with `1.5rem` outer margins and `1rem` gutters.
- **Mobile (320px – 767px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` outer margins and `1rem` gutters. Interactive rows stack vertically to satisfy single-thumb operation.

### Spatial Rhythm
- **Internal Component Density:** Compact, predictable paddings (`space-xs` and `space-sm`) are utilized within data tables, timeline nodes, and badges to preserve structural density.
- **Section Separation:** `space-xl` separates distinct civic chapters, informational panels, and procedural workflow stages, preventing cognitive overload.

## Elevation & Depth

To sustain civic transparency, this system intentionally avoids artificial 3D layering, skeumorphic modeling, or pronounced drop shadows. Depth is achieved via **tonal layering coupled with low-contrast structural boundaries** adapted for dark mode.

### Surface Hierarchy
1. **Base Foundation (Ground):** Dark primary work surfaces, document previews, and application forms.
2. **Structural Grouping (Recessed):** Section backing, filter sidebars, and informational troughs.
3. **Elevated Panels (Floating/Active):** Paired with a sharp 1px border and a diffused, low-opacity ambient shadow.
4. **Transient Flyouts & Modals:** Sharp structural definition utilizing a double boundary—a 1px hairline border supported by a broad ambient floor shadow.

## Shapes

The geometric identity balances civic tradition with modern UI cleanliness through controlled, subtle curvatures.

### Curvature Values (Level 2: Rounded)
- **Base Components (`rounded` / 0.5rem / 8px):** Inputs, standard buttons, notification cards, and dialog panels.
- **Grouping Containers (`rounded-lg` / 1rem / 16px):** Major civic cards, institutional document modules, summary dashboards, and modal dialogs.
- **Hero Containers (`rounded-xl` / 1.5rem / 24px):** Citizen portal hero sections and historical timeline highlight containers.
- **Badges & Interactive Chips:** Retain a structural `rounded` (8px) or complete pill form (`rounded-full`) exclusively for contextual tags and status indicators to differentiate them from actionable buttons.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#E72421`, text `#FFFFFF`, 8px radius, height 44px (touch-compliant). Active state `#C71B18`. Hover state features a subtle 1px border shift rather than dramatic scale.
- **Secondary:** Border 1px solid, text color adjusted for dark mode contrast.
- **Tertiary/Ghost:** Transparent ground, text `#E72421`, font weight 600. Focus ring: 2px offset with 2px solid `#E72421`.

### Status Badges & Chips
- **Civic Status Badges:** Structured capsules featuring 12px label typography, 4px horizontal padding, and low-saturation backgrounds paired with high-contrast text.
- **Filter Chips:** 32px height, 8px radius. When active, `#E72421` background with text.

### Form Fields & Inputs
- **Text Inputs:** Height 44px, border 1px solid, font size 16px to prevent automated zoom on mobile browsers.
- **States:** Focus replaces the border with 2px solid `#E72421` with no offset glow. Error state uses 1.5px solid `#CC4C40`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- **Dimensions:** 20px by 20px with structural border. Checkboxes have a 4px corner radius; radios are circular.
- **Active State:** `#E72421` fill containing an interior checkmark or dot.

### Civic Cards & Information Panels
- **Structure:** Border 1px solid, 16px corner radius (`rounded-lg`), padding `1.5rem`.
- **Header Structure:** Includes an institutional blue kicker label in uppercase (11px, `label-sm`), followed by a `headline-sm` title.

### Historical Timelines & Trackers
- **Stem & Node:** Central or left-aligned 2px continuous track. Milestone nodes utilize an 18px concentric circle with a 3px border in `#E72421` for completed steps, or `#006DC6` for historical anchors.
- **Content Blocks:** Offset by `1.5rem` from the axis, held inside a subtle card to clearly articulate sequence, dates, and civic outcomes.
